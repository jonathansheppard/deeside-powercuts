About these figures

Current cuts come from SP Energy Networks' power cut service. We check every 5 minutes, and a change can take a couple of minutes more to appear here. Deeside.com runs this page independently of SP Energy Networks, so for anything urgent check with them directly or call 105.

The record covers unplanned cuts only. Durations run from when SP Energy Networks logged a cut to when it was marked restored, so power may have gone off later or come back sooner for some homes. A few end times are when we noticed the restoration, up to a few minutes late.

CH4 to CH8 postcodes cross the county boundary in places. A cut covering two postcode areas counts once in each area, and once in the overall totals.
